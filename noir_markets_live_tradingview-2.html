How to read the explanation

The technical-analysis panels below show TradingView's live technical rating and indicator breakdown for this symbol. Use the intraday panel for a shorter-term view and the daily panel for broader direction. The rating is an indicator summary, not a guarantee or a probability of a winning trade.

  • Bullish / Buy: the technical indicators shown lean toward buying.
  • Bearish / Sell: the technical indicators shown lean toward selling.
  • Neutral: the indicator signals are mixed or do not show a strong directional lean.
Live-data transparency: These panels are embedded directly from TradingView. Forex and crypto widget data is generally real-time; stock/exchange-related data may be delayed due to exchange licensing. TradingView widgets do not expose their underlying values to this page through a public data API, so this page does not invent a separate bias, explanation, or confidence percentage. The technical-rating panel includes its own moving-average and oscillator breakdown.
